[ 03 // Spatial Intelligence & Computer Vision ]

Visual SLAM · 3D Reconstruction · Point Cloud Registration & Segmentation @IIT Jodhpur

At IIT Jodhpur, I worked on 3D reconstruction using monocular and stereo video frames, exploring Visual SLAM (Simultaneous Localization and Mapping) and point cloud registration and segmentation. The work involved reconstructing 3D environments from visual data, aligning point clouds, and segmenting the resulting scenes to better understand their spatial structure. This experience allowed me to explore how physical spaces can be represented digitally, combining computer vision, 3D mapping, and spatial analysis.
